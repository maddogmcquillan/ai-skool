Learn AI

For parents and guardians

How Learn AI works, how we keep it safe, and what we ask of you.

What your child gets

Learn AI is an online after-school program for ages 11 to 17. Members watch short video classes, build a project after each one, share what they made, and ask questions in a moderated community. Seventeen classes cover how AI works, how to use it well, and ten projects: a game, an app, a website, a chatbot, an AI agent, a video, a cartoon, a song, AI art and a comic. Adults get their own courses in the same membership.

How we keep it safe

  • No private messaging. Members cannot message each other. Everything happens in public spaces the team can see and moderate.
  • A person reads every thread. Coach, our AI helper, answers questions first, grounded in the class material. An adult on the team reviews every conversation and steps in when needed. Coach never asks for personal details, and it hands billing, account and safety topics to a person.
  • No personal details. Community rules forbid sharing full names, schools, addresses, phone numbers, social media handles and photos of people. Coach refuses them too. Members can report anything with one click.
  • Consent first. When a student joins we ask for a parent or guardian email and a consent checkbox. Students must be 13 or older to hold their own account; for 11 and 12 year olds a parent creates and holds the account and does the program alongside them.
  • Public profiles stay small. Member emails are hidden from other members, and the community is not indexed by search engines.

What we collect about your child

The email used to sign in, a display name, whether the member is a student, parent or adult learner, your parent or guardian email, and what they post inside the community. Payment details go to Stripe and never touch us. The full list is in the privacy policy.

Tools used in class

Most projects use tools with a free tier. Two do not: the AI art class uses Midjourney, which is paid, and the video class may need a paid ChatGPT plan. Each class says so up front. We recommend that a parent creates any third-party accounts.

Billing and cancelling

The Founding Member plan is $49 a month for one student, billed monthly, and the rate stays the same for as long as you remain a member. Cancel any time from your account settings, in two clicks, or by emailing us; access continues to the end of the month you have paid for. Billing questions always go to a person: email hello@joinlearnai.com.

How to reach a human

Email hello@joinlearnai.com, or post in Get Unstuck and write that you would like a person to reply. Safety concerns are read the same day.

Consent statement shown at signup: "I am the parent or legal guardian of this student, I have read the community rules and privacy policy, and I consent to their participation in Learn AI."
