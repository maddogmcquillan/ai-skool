Common questions

Is this live, or self-paced?
Self-paced. Every class is on demand, so your kid can start tonight and watch when it suits your family. When they get stuck, they post in Get Unstuck. Coach answers in minutes, and a real person reads every thread.
What ages is this for?
Built for 11 to 17. Middle schoolers do best with a parent watching the first classes with them. High schoolers usually run with it on their own.
Does my kid need to know how to code?
No. Every project uses tools that build from a description of what you want. Kids who like code can go deeper, but nothing requires it.
What do they need?
A laptop or Chromebook and an internet connection. Most tools have a free tier. Where a class uses a paid tool, the class says so before anyone signs up for anything.
Is it safe?
Kids can't message each other. Everything is in spaces the team can see. A parent or guardian email and consent are required before a student can join. The rules and Coach both refuse full names, schools, addresses and social handles.
How do I pause or cancel?
