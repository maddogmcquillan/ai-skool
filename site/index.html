Built for parents to trust

A community designed around kids, not adapted to them.

We run this the way we would want a program for our own kids run. Here is exactly how.

  • No private messaging. Members cannot message each other. Everything happens in public spaces the team can see.
  • A person reads every thread. Coach, our AI helper, answers first. An adult on the team reviews every conversation and steps in when needed.
  • Parent consent at signup. We ask for a parent or guardian email and consent before a student can join.
  • No personal details, ever. Rules and the AI helper both refuse full names, schools, addresses, phone numbers and social handles.
  • A Parent Hub. How the community runs, what the classes cover, and how to help at home.
  • Humans handle money. Billing, refunds and account questions always go to a person, never to the AI.

Questions parents ask

The short answers.

What ages is this for?

Built for 11 to 17. Younger kids can do it with a parent alongside. Adults get their own courses in the same membership.

Does my kid need to know how to code?

No. Every project uses tools that let you build by describing what you want. Kids who like code can go deeper, but nothing requires it.

What do they need?

A laptop or Chromebook and an internet connection. Most tools have a free tier. One art class uses Midjourney, which is paid, and the video class may need a paid ChatGPT plan. We say so in the class before anyone signs up for anything.

Is it live or self-paced?

Self-paced classes, plus a weekly live build-along and Q&A. Questions get answered any day in Ask Coach.

How much time does it take?

About two hours a week is a good pace: one class and its project. Faster or slower is fine.

Who is Coach?

Coach is an AI helper that answers questions inside the community, grounded in the class material. It never asks for personal details and it hands anything about billing, accounts or safety to a person on our team. A human reviews every thread.

How do I cancel?

From your account settings, in two clicks, any time. You keep access until the end of the billing period. Billing questions go to a person; email us and we will sort it out.
