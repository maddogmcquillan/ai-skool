Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Draft prepared for review by a lawyer before launch. Items in brackets are decisions for the owner.

1. What this covers

This policy explains what Learn AI ("we", "us") collects from visitors to joinlearnai.com and members of the community at www.joinlearnai.com, and what we do with it. Our community includes minors, so we collect as little as we can.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: the email used to sign in, a display name, and whether the member is a student, parent or adult learner.
  • Parent or guardian email and consent, collected when a student joins.
  • What members post in the community: questions, comments, projects and reactions.
  • Payment: handled by Stripe. We see the amount, the plan and the last four digits of the card; we never see or store the full card number.
  • Usage: pages visited, classes watched and standard device information such as browser type and IP address, collected by the community platform and by our website analytics.

3. How we use it

  • To run the membership: sign-in, access to classes, billing and support.
  • To answer questions: posts in the Ask Coach space are sent to an AI service to draft a reply. The AI provider does not use this content to train its models. A person on our team reviews the conversations.
  • To keep the community safe: moderation, rule enforcement and responding to reports.
  • To measure our advertising: our website and checkout use the Meta Pixel and Meta's Conversions API so we can tell which ads led to a purchase. We share a hashed email and the purchase amount with Meta for that purpose. We do not run ads aimed at children and we do not share what children post with advertisers.

4. Who we share it with

Only companies that help us run the Service, under contracts that limit what they can do with it: Circle (the community platform), Stripe (payments), Railway (hosting for our support tools), Anthropic (the AI service behind Coach) and Meta (advertising measurement). We do not sell personal information.

5. Children's privacy

Members must be 13 or older to hold an account, and members under 18 join with a parent or guardian's consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if we learn that we have, we delete it. Parents can review or delete their child's information, or withdraw consent, by emailing hello@joinlearnai.com. Members are told not to post personal details, and our moderation removes them when they appear.

6. Cookies and tracking

Our website uses the Meta Pixel, which sets cookies to measure ads. You can limit this in your browser settings and in your Meta ad preferences. The community platform uses cookies needed for sign-in and preferences.

7. Keeping and deleting information

We keep account information while the membership is active and for [12] months after it ends, then delete or anonymise it, except where we must keep records for tax or legal reasons. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

8. Your choices and rights

You can see, correct or delete your information, or ask us not to process it, by emailing hello@joinlearnai.com. Residents of some places, including California, have specific rights under local law; we honour them. We will answer within 30 days.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to member information is limited to the people who need it to run the Service. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you as the law requires.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the date at the top. Questions or requests: hello@joinlearnai.com. Postal address: [business address].
