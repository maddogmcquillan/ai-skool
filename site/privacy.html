Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

1. What this covers

This policy explains what Learn AI ("we", "us") collects from visitors to joinlearnai.com and from members of the community at www.joinlearnai.com, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Our community includes minors, so we collect as little as we can and we never sell personal information.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: the email used to sign in, a display name, and whether the member is a student, parent or adult learner.
  • Parent or guardian email and consent, collected when a student joins.
  • What members post in the community: questions, comments, projects, reactions and reports.
  • Payment information: collected and stored by Stripe, our payment processor. We see the amount, the plan, the billing name and the last four digits of the card. We never see or store the full card number.
  • Usage and device information: pages visited, classes watched, browser type, IP address and similar technical data, collected by the community platform and by the analytics described below.
  • Messages you send us by email or through the community.

3. How we use it

  • To run the membership: sign-in, access to classes, billing, support and account notices.
  • To answer questions: posts in the Get Unstuck space are sent to Anthropic, an AI service, to draft a reply from Coach. That provider processes the text to produce the answer and, under our agreement with it, does not use it to train its models. A person on our team reviews the conversations.
  • To keep the community safe: moderation, enforcing the community rules, responding to reports and protecting members.
  • To measure advertising: our website and checkout use the Meta Pixel and Meta's Conversions API so we can tell which ads led to a purchase. For that purpose we share the purchase amount and a hashed (scrambled) version of the purchaser's email with Meta. We do not run ads directed at children and we do not share what children post with advertisers.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax records, and to establish or defend legal claims.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with companies that help us run the Service, under agreements that limit what they can do with it: Circle (the community platform), Stripe (payments), Railway (hosting for our support tools), Anthropic (the AI service behind Coach) and Meta (advertising measurement). We may also share information when the law requires it or to protect the safety of members. We do not sell personal information.

5. Children's privacy

Members must be 13 or older to hold an account, and members under 18 join with a parent or guardian's consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete it. Members are told not to post personal details, and moderation removes them when they appear. Parents and guardians can review their child's information, ask us to delete it, or withdraw consent at any time by emailing hello@joinlearnai.com; we may ask you to verify that you are the parent first.

6. Cookies and tracking

The community platform uses cookies that are needed for sign-in and remembering your preferences. Our website and checkout use the Meta Pixel, which sets cookies to measure advertising. You can limit or block cookies in your browser, and you can adjust ad settings in your Meta account. Blocking cookies may affect sign-in.

7. How long we keep information

We keep account information while the membership is active and for as long as we need it afterwards to provide support, keep required business records and resolve disputes, then we delete or anonymise it. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you or your child, or ask us to stop using it, by emailing hello@joinlearnai.com. We answer within 30 days and never treat you differently for making a request.

California residents. California law gives you the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use and share it (this policy), the right to delete it, the right to correct it, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information. Our use of the Meta Pixel on the marketing site may count as "sharing" under California law; to opt out, email hello@joinlearnai.com with "Do not share" in the subject, or block the pixel in your browser. We do not knowingly share the personal information of anyone under 16. We also honour Global Privacy Control signals where our platform supports them.

9. Security

Information is encrypted in transit, payment data is handled entirely by Stripe, and access to member information is limited to the people who need it to run the Service. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the new date at the top; for material changes we will tell members by email or in the community. Questions or requests: hello@joinlearnai.com.
