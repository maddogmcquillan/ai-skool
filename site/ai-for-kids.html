Questions parents ask

What ages is this for?

Built for 11 to 17. Younger kids can do it with a parent alongside. Adults get their own courses in the same membership.

Does my kid need to know how to code?

No. Every project uses tools that build from a description of what you want. Kids who like code can go deeper, but nothing requires it.

What do they need?

A laptop or Chromebook and an internet connection. Most tools have a free tier. One art class uses Midjourney, which is paid, and the video class may need a paid ChatGPT plan. We say so in the class before anyone signs up for anything.

How much time does it take?

About two hours a week is a good pace: one class and its project. Self-paced, plus a weekly live build-along and Q&A.

How do I cancel?

From your account settings, in two clicks, any time. You keep access until the end of the billing period. Billing questions go to a person; email us and we will sort it out.
