Questions parents ask

What ages is this for?

Built for 11 to 17. Middle schoolers do best with a parent watching the first classes alongside; high schoolers usually run with it on their own.

Does my kid need to know how to code?

No. Every project uses tools that build from a description of what you want. Kids who like code can go deeper, but nothing requires it.

What do they need?

A laptop or Chromebook and an internet connection. Most tools have a free tier. Where a class uses a paid tool, the class says so before anyone signs up for anything.

How much time does it take?

About two hours a week is a good pace: one class and its project. Everything is self-paced, so a busy week just means picking up where they left off.

Is this a replacement for school?

No. It's the part school isn't covering yet: how AI works, and how to make things with it. The habits carry straight back into schoolwork.

How do I cancel?

From your account settings, any time. You keep access until the end of the billing period. Billing questions go to a person; email us and we will sort it out.
