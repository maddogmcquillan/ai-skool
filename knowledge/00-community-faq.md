# Learn AI community FAQ

> EDIT ME. This file is always given to the bot. Keep it short and accurate.

## What is Learn AI?
Learn AI is an online after-school program that teaches kids aged 11 to 17 how AI works and how to build things with it. There are also courses for adults. Everything lives in this community: video lessons, projects, live sessions, and a place to ask questions.

## How the Classroom is organized
One Classroom course with three sections. Beginner AI has three lessons: What is AI?, Generative AI in a Nutshell, and ChatGPT Power User. Do them in order first. Intermediate AI has four lessons: Large Language Models Explained, The Perfect ChatGPT Prompt Formula, What are AI Agents?, and AI Agents Fundamentals. Do them in order after Beginner. Project Classes are ten standalone builds you can do in any order: Make a Game, an App, a Website, a Chatbot, an AI Agent, a Video, a Cartoon, Music, AI Art, and a Comic. Each lesson is a hand-picked video from an expert creator, credited in the lesson, plus our notes and a project.

## Which tools cost money
Most project classes use tools with a free tier. Midjourney, used in Make AI Art, is paid with no free tier. Sora may require a paid ChatGPT plan. Ask a parent before signing up for anything.

## Where to ask questions
Post in the Ask Coach space. Coach, the AI helper, replies within a few minutes. A real person on the team reads every thread and steps in when needed.

## How to request a new course
Post one idea per post in the Course Requests space. Like the posts you agree with. The most-liked requests get built first.

## Community rules for members
Be kind. No sharing of personal information like your full name, school, address, phone number, or social media. No asking to meet up. Member-to-member direct messages are turned off on purpose. Report anything that feels wrong using the report button, or tell a parent.

## Membership, billing and account questions
These are handled by the team, not by Coach. Post in Ask Coach and a person will reply, or email the support address on the website.
