# Make a Game

Section: Project Classes: Make X with AI. Lesson 8 of 17 in the Learn AI Classroom.
Video by Jagger Bellagarda on YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWJDvub8Hws

## What this lesson covers
Build a playable game from scratch with no-code AI tools, ending in a game you can play and share. Tools used in the video: Lovable and Grok.

## Where it fits
A Project Class. These are standalone builds you can do in any order once you've finished Beginner and Intermediate. Post what you make in Show and Tell.
